NucMonitor is a single place to answer "what is happening at, or near, a US nuclear site today": which reactors are running, what the NRC recorded, what hazards are nearby, what radiation monitors see, and what the agencies and the industry are saying. It started as a fork of World Monitor and now ships only this dashboard.

Run it locally with `npm run dev`; build it with `npm run build`.

## The facility registry

`src/config/us-nuclear-facilities.ts` is a pure data module with no imports, so the browser, node tests, seed scripts, and the MCP surface can all read it. Every entry carries an `id`, `name`, `category`, `status`, state, coordinates, operator, regulator, and optional NRC docket, unit list, ISFSI flag, reference URL, notes, and `statusAliases` (used to match the NRC status report's unit labels).

Categories:

- `power-reactor`: 55 sites with operating units, including Vogtle 3 and 4 and the Palisades restart.
- `restart`: Crane Clean Energy Center (TMI-1) and Duane Arnold.
- `new-build`: Natrium (Kemmerer), Kairos Hermes, X-energy Long Mott, ACU MSRR, TVA Clinch River BWRX-300, Holtec SMR-300, Oklo Aurora, Fermi America, and the abandoned V.C. Summer 2 and 3.
- `decommissioning`: permanently shut sites, ISFSI-only sites, and entombed demonstration reactors.
- `doe-site`: national laboratories, NNSA production sites, naval reactors laboratories, and Environmental Management and Legacy Management sites.
- `fuel-cycle`: enrichment, conversion, fuel fabrication, and the TRISO and laser enrichment projects in licensing.
- `research-reactor`: NRC-licensed university, government, and industrial research reactors.
- `uranium-recovery`: the operating conventional mill and in-situ recovery projects.
- `waste`: WIPP, Yucca Mountain, the two licensed consolidated interim storage facilities, commercial low-level waste sites, and GE Morris.

`US_NUCLEAR_REGISTRY_AS_OF` records the review date. Capacities are net summer MWe; license years are current NRC operating license expirations (renewed or subsequent renewed where granted). Verify a value against the linked NRC or DOE page before citing it. `summarizeUsNuclearFleet()` derives fleet statistics and `matchNrcUnitName()` resolves an NRC status label such as `Saint Lucie 2` to a facility and unit.

Sources used to compile the registry:

- [NRC operating reactor list](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/operating/list-power-reactor-units.html) and [facility locator](https://www.nrc.gov/info-finder/region-state/)
- [NRC power reactor decommissioning](https://www.nrc.gov/info-finder/decommissioning/power-reactor/), [research and test reactors](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/non-power/), [fuel cycle facilities](https://www.nrc.gov/materials/fuel-cycle-fac.html), [uranium recovery](https://www.nrc.gov/materials/uranium-recovery.html), [new reactors](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/new-reactors.html)
- [DOE Environmental Management sites](https://www.energy.gov/em/mission-and-sites) and [NNSA locations](https://www.energy.gov/nnsa/nnsa-locations)
- [EIA nuclear](https://www.eia.gov/nuclear/) and [IAEA PRIS](https://pris.iaea.org/PRIS/CountryStatistics/CountryDetails.aspx?current=US)

## NRC reactor status feed

The NRC publishes a pipe-delimited bulk file, `PowerReactorStatusForLast365Days.txt`, with one row per unit per calendar day (`ReportDt|Unit|Power|Reason|Change|...`). nrc.gov sends no CORS headers, so `api/nrc-reactor-status.js` passes the file through as `text/plain` with a header check, an 8 MB cap, and a 30-minute shared cache. It is listed in `api/api-route-exceptions.json` as an `upstream-proxy`.

`src/shared/nrc-reactor-status.ts` parses the rows by header name (column order can change upstream), builds the latest-day snapshot with previous-day lookups, and summarizes fleet power. `src/services/nrc-reactor-status.ts` fetches through the proxy with a circuit breaker and enriches each unit with the registry record. The panel refreshes every 30 minutes while near the viewport (`REFRESH_INTERVALS.nrcReactorStatus`).

Live verification of the proxy against nrc.gov still needs to happen from a deployment that can reach nrc.gov; the parser is covered by fixtures in `tests/nrc-reactor-status.test.mts`.

## All-hazards correlation

`src/services/nuclear-facility-hazards.ts` is pure: `correlateFacilityHazards(inputs, options)` takes the feeds the dashboard already loads and returns one report per facility with every signal inside its watch radius, a severity, and a ranking score. Defaults:

| Feed | Radius | Window | Severity rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| USGS earthquakes | 100 km | 72 h | M6+, or M5+ within 50 km, is critical; M5+, or M4+ within 50 km, is high |
| NWS alerts | polygon containment, or 60 km from the centroid when no polygon | until `expires` | Extreme or hurricane / tornado / flash-flood-emergency warnings are critical; Severe or any warning is high |
| NASA FIRMS fires | 25 km | current detections | collapsed to one signal per site; distance, peak FRP, and count set severity |
| RadNet / Safecast | 80 km | current anomalies | corroborated spikes are critical |
| NHC / EONET storms | 250 km, measured to the closest forecast-track point | open events | category 3+ within 150 km is critical |

A listing is a prompt to read the linked source and the NRC event notification reports, not a claim that the site is affected. The panel re-correlates whenever `data-loader` pushes a new earthquake, weather, fire, radiation, or natural-event batch.

## Map layer

`usNuclearFacilities` is a new `MapLayers` key with DeckGL and SVG renderers, a curated layer explanation card, a command palette toggle, and a popup (`usNuclear`) showing category, operator, regulator and NRC region, units, notes, reference links, and docket links. The global `nuclear` layer (worldwide sites) stays available but off by default.

## Disclaimer

NucMonitor is informational. It republishes public data and is not an official NRC notification channel; always confirm against the linked source.
