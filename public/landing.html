US nuclear facility all-hazards monitoring

What is happening at, or near, every US nuclear site, on one screen.

NucMonitor joins NRC reactor status and event notifications with the weather, earthquake, wildfire, storm, outage and radiation signals inside each site's 10- and 50-mile emergency planning zones. Every status is explainable and links back to its public source.

NucMonitor is informational. It republishes public data from the NRC, DOE, EIA, EPA, USGS, NWS, NOAA and NASA and is not an official NRC notification channel. Hazard statuses are a proximity screen computed from public feeds, not a statement about plant condition. Confirm anything you act on against the linked source. The registry contains only publicly documented facility information.
