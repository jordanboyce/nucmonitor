Privacy

Last updated 20 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-20

Placeholder privacy notice for the NucMonitor demonstration deployment. It describes what the software does today and will be replaced by a reviewed notice before any partner pilot.

What the public demo collects

The public demo does not require an account. The server keeps ordinary request logs (IP address, user agent, requested path, timestamp) for abuse prevention and rate limiting, for a short period. Usage analytics are off unless the deployment operator has configured a self-hosted analytics instance, in which case page views and feature usage are recorded without cookies. Error reports are sent to an error-tracking service only when the operator has configured one; they contain stack traces and the page URL, with query parameters redacted.

What stays in your browser

Your watchlist of sites, panel layout, theme and similar preferences are stored in your browser's local storage and are not sent to the server unless you sign in to a partner deployment and turn on preference sync.

Partner deployments

Partner deployments use an identity provider for sign-in. We receive your name, work email address and a user identifier, and we store which legal version you accepted and when. Access requests submitted through the home page are stored (email, organisation, role and message) so we can respond.

Data processing

Facility and hazard data shown on the dashboard are public records republished from federal and open sources; they contain no personal data. Personal data we hold is processed to run the service, respond to requests and prevent abuse, and is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Your rights and contact

You can ask what we hold about you and ask for it to be deleted by writing to the contact address on the home page. Partner organisations that need a data processing agreement should ask for one.

NucMonitor is informational. It republishes public data from the NRC, DOE, EIA, EPA, USGS, NWS, NOAA and NASA and is not an official NRC notification channel.
