1. What NucMonitor is

NucMonitor is an informational dashboard that collects publicly available data about United States nuclear facilities and the hazards near them: NRC reactor status reports and event notifications, National Weather Service alerts, USGS earthquakes, NASA FIRMS fire detections, National Hurricane Center and EONET events, EPA RadNet and Safecast radiation readings, and news feeds. It presents that data in one place with distances to each site's emergency planning zones.

2. Not an official channel

NucMonitor is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official notification channel of the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission, the Department of Energy, or any facility operator. Hazard statuses are a proximity screen computed from public feeds, not a statement about plant condition. Always confirm anything you act on against the linked source.

3. Demonstration data
